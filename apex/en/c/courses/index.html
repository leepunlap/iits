Course Series

The IITS AI curriculum

IITS is the schools AI education brand of China MIIT Publishing and Media Group. It is built for use inside schools, and combines general-education courses, an AI laboratory, after-school provision, and assessments and competitions into one framework. It was developed by university specialists together with school educators, uses the assessments as its exit qualification, and spans primary through senior secondary.

The course framework

The courses cover programming, computing, engineering and science, from the youngest years to senior secondary, in three strands: programming, artificial intelligence, and science and technology literacy. Each strand offers teaching, activities and competitions, taking a student from first encounter with AI, through general fluency, to advanced work — building logical thinking and problem-solving alongside imagination and the pleasure of making something.

Opening young minds, enabling what comes next

The framework is built around core competencies and follows how children actually develop: it starts from everyday situations, leans on hands-on practice, and draws across subjects. The aim is practical AI skill, inventive thinking, a critical eye and a sense of responsibility — offered as a system that other schools can adopt and adapt.

Technology and maker teachers in primary and secondary schools, teaching staff at education and training organisations, and students on AI-related courses.

2. IITS AI teacher training

Covering the IITS AI framework, teaching AI to students, and teachers' own development.

IITS is the AI education brand of China MIIT Publishing and Media Group, built around general-education courses, a laboratory, after-school provision, and assessments and competitions, and designed for AI teaching in schools. Its focus is digital competence, computational thinking and inventiveness, delivered through a standard course framework and teacher training that supports Hong Kong teachers directly. Training falls into three parts.

Student AI literacy and skills. The courses spiral upward, from everyday examples to cross-subject application, concentrating on computational thinking and digital competence. General foundation: how AI works, hands-on experience of it, and practical skills. The K series, designed for after-school provision, brings thinking, skills and building together with its own hardware. Classroom activities and worked product examples are written to hold interest as well as teach, and students can show what they have learned through recognised routes — the Youth Artificial Intelligence Technology Proficiency Test, the Youth Information Technology Innovation activities, and the World Robot Contest.

Professional development. The aim is to lower the barrier to teaching this material, so that a teacher without an IT background can pick it up quickly: detailed standard lesson plans and slides that cut preparation time; guidance on using AI in everyday teaching and assessment; and the IITS management platform, which puts teaching resources one click away.

Support for Hong Kong teachers. IITS Hong Kong and the Hong Kong branch support schools running AI training and setting up an AI Wonder Lab, supplying lesson plans, slides and build instructions detailed enough to teach from directly.

Course length is set to the school's or organisation's requirements, from half a day to five days. Certificates are issued jointly by the Hong Kong branch of the Youth Information Technology Training Project and IITS Hong Kong.

Study Tours

About the study tours

IITS study tours are built on three things held together — study, enquiry and travel — so that each student comes away having learned something, thought about something, and enjoyed it.

A study tour is not a trip so much as a stretch of growing up: an ambition formed while exploring a technology, confidence gained through a cultural encounter. The tours combine university courses, research practice and company visits with competition and cultural immersion, broadening scientific literacy, inventive thinking and international outlook together.

As an extension of the classroom, study tours widen the path a child grows along. IITS runs a series of themed tours built around science, technology and making, so that young people have more ways to explore.

Competitions

Competition programme

The AI competition series brings new energy to youth science and technology education.

1. Competition support

Complete event packages for ages 4–15 — format, equipment and platform — giving a route from district and municipal rounds through provincial level to nationally recognised competitions.

Entry routes to several competitions on the Ministry of Education's approved list: the Youth Science and Technology Education Achievement Exhibition, the World Robot Contest, and the National Youth Communications Technology Innovation Competition.

A full professional event-operations service.

2. Regional competitions

Regional rounds are run in partnership with local authorities and science associations across the mainland, and provincial youth science competitions are held in Hunan, Yunnan, Jiangsu, Guangdong, Guizhou and Sichuan.

Regional events are announced by their own local education authorities. Entries for Hong Kong students are arranged by this branch — please contact us for information.
