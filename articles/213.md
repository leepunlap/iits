<p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: center; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="font-size: 24px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; color: #000000;"><strong><span style="font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 24px; letter-spacing: 0px;">Youth Information Technology Training Project</span></strong></span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;"><strong><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif;">I. Basic Introduction</span></strong></span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">“The Youth Information Technology Training Project” was jointly initiated by the Education and Examination Center of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the China Electronic Education Society, and the China Industry and Information Technology Publishing and Media Group—Beijing Xintong Media Co., Ltd. It aims to cultivate teenagers&#39; capabilities in artificial intelligence, science and technology, and human-machine collaboration, providing them with competency certification in these fields through assessments or activities. Key projects include: the Youth Artificial Intelligence Technology Proficiency Test, the Youth Artificial Intelligence Programming Proficiency Test, the Youth Screen-Free Programming Proficiency Test, the Youth Science and Technology Literacy Test, and the Youth Drone Technology Proficiency Test.</span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">&nbsp;</span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;"><strong><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">II. Project Background</span></strong></span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">The implementation of The Youth Information Technology Training Project&nbsp;strictly adheres to national policies on science and technology education, artificial intelligence education, and information literacy enhancement. The State Council&#39;s &quot;New Generation Artificial Intelligence Development Plan&quot; explicitly mandates the introduction of AI-related courses in primary and secondary schools, gradual promotion of programming education, and the advancement of universal intelligent education. The Ministry of Education&#39;s &quot;Compulsory Education Information Technology Curriculum Standards&quot; designate information technology as a national compulsory curriculum, incorporating programming, algorithms, artificial intelligence, and data security into mandatory teaching content to comprehensively strengthen adolescents&#39; digital literacy and innovative thinking. As the education digitalization strategy advances, AI literacy education and practical technological skills development have become pivotal components of quality-oriented education in the modern era. Grounded in authoritative policy guidance, this program establishes a scientifically structured training and assessment system to equip adolescents with the competencies required for the digital age and cultivate future talent for building a technologically advanced nation.</span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">&nbsp;</span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;"><strong><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">III. Significance of the Project</span></strong></span></p><p style="margin: 0px; text-indent: 0px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; background: rgb(255, 255, 255); line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="letter-spacing: 0px; font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">The implementation of The Youth Information Technology Training Project&nbsp;holds significant practical importance for fostering students &#39;holistic growth. In terms of student development, the program is grounded in adolescents&#39; cognitive patterns, helping them cultivate scientific digital literacy and information ethics awareness while enhancing comprehensive abilities in independent thinking, self-directed inquiry, and innovative creation—laying a solid foundation for adapting to learning and daily life in the intelligent era. Regarding skill acquisition, the program focuses on core competencies such as programming thinking, algorithm application, foundational artificial intelligence, and problem-solving, improving students &#39;logical reasoning, hands-on practical skills, and collaborative innovation capabilities, thereby transforming information technology knowledge into applicable and demonstrable practical skills. Additionally, the program features a tiered assessment system that provides phased evaluations of students&#39; programming and IT proficiency, clearly reflecting learning outcomes, identifying weak areas, and offering scientific guidance for future learning plans—thereby supporting adolescents&#39; steady growth in the digital age.</span></p><p style="line-height: 1.75em;"><span style="font-family: arial, helvetica, sans-serif; font-size: 16px;">&nbsp;</span></p><p><br/></p>
