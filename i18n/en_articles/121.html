Three group standards for the Youth IT Training Project were formally published on 19 October at the China Electronics Education Association's thirtieth anniversary academic conference, held at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications under the theme "Digital intelligence for high-quality electronics education".

The standards are:

  • Technical and Skills Standard for the Youth AI Programming Proficiency Test (YCL)
  • Technical and Skills Standard for the Youth AI Technology Proficiency Test (YAI)
  • Technical and Skills Standard for Youth Screen-free Programming (ECL)

They were published jointly by Ms Ma Qiang, President of the China Electronics Education Association; Mr Guo Baolong, Vice President; Mr Tan Zhibin, Head of the Training Division at the MIIT Education and Examination Center, and his deputy Mr Xian Ruping; and Mr Fu Fangming and Mr Yang Rong of the Association's Youth Education Branch.

The standards were initiated by the Association and developed under the MIIT Education and Examination Center and the Youth Education Branch, to strengthen the authority and rigour of the assessment programme.

On 12 October the MIIT Education and Examination Center convened a panel to review the training course standards, examining their framework, content and implementation. The panel judged them well founded, clearly organised and structurally sound, and approved the course standards for Youth AI Technology (robotics) and Youth AI Technology (screen-free programming).

Together the standards are intended to raise young people's information technology literacy and their ability to apply AI techniques in practice.
