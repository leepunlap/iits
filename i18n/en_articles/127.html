The "Future Industry and IT Talent" youth science literacy programme was launched on 19 October at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

Attending were Ms Ma Qiang, President of the China Electronics Education Association; Mr Xian Ruping, Deputy Head of the Training Division at the MIIT Education and Examination Center; Mr Guo Baolong, Vice President of the Association; and Mr Fu Fangming and Mr Yang Rong of the Association's Youth Education Branch and Beijing Xintong Media. Dozens of primary and secondary students took part.

The programme was planned by the Youth Education Branch to mark the Association's thirtieth anniversary. It is intended both to develop the Branch's framework for science and technology education and to give young people a platform on which to show what they can do.

Ms Ma Qiang set out four hopes for the students: to hold to a large ambition and a sense of technological innovation; to study hard and raise their own capability; to practise boldly and train themselves to think creatively; and to work together.

Mr Xian Ruping spoke of building consensus and broadening cooperation between the parties involved, to identify and develop more talent for the industry and information technology sector.

Professor Zhao Bo, Deputy Head of the Automation Department at the university's School of Automation and School of Artificial Intelligence, gave a talk titled "From Rules to Statistics: Exploring Artificial Intelligence", tracing how the field developed. Students took an active part in the question-and-answer session.

Three students presented their project work, and guests presented commemorative certificates.

The programme is organised by the Youth Education Branch of the China Electronics Education Association under the theme "Practice raises science literacy; become the industry and IT talent of the future". It has four strands — AI exploration, programming challenges, intelligent creation, and creative application. Entries are made by region, with schools or individuals registering and forming teams; finished work is published on the programme website and participants receive certificates.
